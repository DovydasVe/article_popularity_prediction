With the ever-increasing availability of media in different sources and formats, the
battle for consumer attention is at its highest. Often, users will only look at content for a
handful of seconds before their attention moves elsewhere. In this project, we intend to
implement machine learning models to predict the most popular news items on any given day
to be presented on the front page of news outlets. We implemented three different models
under four different conditions (a total of 12 models). We score these models with a
customised scoring function we have created. The best model is the baseline XGBoost with a
precision score of 0.28 on the customised test set.
